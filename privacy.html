Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

At NovaaForgee Systems ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal and technical data collected through our applications and developer tools.

1. Information We Access & Collect

Our automation tools and applications may access:

  • API access tokens explicitly provided by users to perform authorized actions.
  • Basic media and post content metadata required to schedule or publish pins and visual media.
  • System logs necessary for debugging API error rates and service status.

2. How We Use Data

We only use the data to perform the core operations requested by the authenticated account holder:

  • Automating pin publishing, scheduling, and boards management.
  • Reading engagement and analytics reports strictly for performance reporting.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or authentication tokens with any third parties. All credentials and access tokens are handled securely with industry-standard encryption practices.

4. Third-Party Services

Our tools interact directly with authorized platform APIs (such as Pinterest API). Their handling of data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, feel free to contact us via our GitHub organization page at https://github.com/novaaforgee.
